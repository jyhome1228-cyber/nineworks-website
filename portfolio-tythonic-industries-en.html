Project Origin

It began with a small logo I noticed inside the game.

While passing a Blue Chip Transmitter in PUBG, a small TYTHONIC INDUSTRIES mark on the side of the blue industrial unit caught my attention. It was not a piece of information required to play the game, nor a name placed at the center of a quest. Yet the mark kept returning — on research facilities, containers, equipment and objects associated with Blue Chip technology. That repetition raised a simple question: what kind of company is this?

The more I looked, the larger the implied organization became. TYTHONIC is framed as a life-science company founded around the ambition of extending human life, while the wider setting connects it to advanced prosthetics, biotechnology, Blue Chip technology, participant equipment and the technical infrastructure surrounding the Battlegrounds. What was missing was not scale, but articulation: the information existed as fragments rather than as a complete corporate history or identity manual.

The project therefore did not begin by redesigning the logo. It began by preserving what already existed and asking how the empty spaces between those fragments could be made legible. Existing clues were translated into Narrative, Verbal Identity and Corporate Identity, then expanded into the kind of visual and operational system a global corporation would need in order to feel real across documents, laboratories, products, factories and field infrastructure.

What is TYTHONIC INDUSTRIES?The question that started the project.

What is TYTHONIC? / World-building Basis

WE PROTECT LIFE.
WE MANAGE SURVIVAL.

Publicly, TYTHONIC can be read as a life-science corporation built around human longevity, medicine, biotechnology and advanced prosthetics. Inside the Battlegrounds, however, its role appears much broader. Blue Chip technology, participant systems, research facilities, transmitters and industrial infrastructure suggest a company that does not simply sponsor the environment, but helps provide the technical structure that allows it to operate.

This duality becomes the core of the brand. One side speaks in the language of protection, continuity and human progress. The other speaks in identification, registration, connectivity, authorization and control.

PUBLIC FACELife Science

Human longevity, biotechnology, advanced prosthetics and the promise of protecting life.

CORE TECHNOLOGYBlue Chip

A biometric identity technology connecting an individual to a larger continuity system.

OPERATIONAL REALITYSurvival Infrastructure

Research, equipment, communication and field infrastructure connected to the Battlegrounds.

TYTHONIC INDUSTRIES / THE HISTORY BEHIND THE BATTLEGROUNDS

Follow the company’s history,
and the logic behind the Battlegrounds begins to appear.

This story does not begin with a villainous corporation secretly building an arena. It begins with a far more credible ambition: extending human life. But extending life creates another problem — the damaged body must be restored. Restoring the body requires the body to be read. Once the body can be read, the individual becomes identifiable as data. Once identity becomes data, it can be stored, transferred and connected. Eventually, survival itself becomes something a system can register and manage.

TYTHONIC is therefore more unsettling when it is not reduced to a simple evil corporation. Its history is a story about technologies created to protect human life gradually becoming technologies capable of administering it. Across five chapters, Life → Body → Data → Blue Chip → Battlegrounds becomes one continuous research logic.

01 LIFE→02 BODY→03 DATA→04 BLUE CHIP→05 SYSTEM
01
1977 — Extending Life

We wanted to extend life.

Everything begins with research into the possibility of extending human life.

In 1977, TYTHONIC was far removed from the vast technology corporation implied by the Battlegrounds. It could be imagined as a modest life-science organization: low research buildings, limited equipment and a small number of teams focused on ageing, disease and the biological limits of the human body. The first question was deceptively simple — how much longer could a human life continue?

The important shift was not the answer, but the way the company framed the question. Life was no longer treated as an untouchable mystery. It could be observed, measured and, if sufficiently understood, modified. This belief becomes the oldest principle running through the company’s later technologies.

Nothing is visibly sinister yet. In fact, TYTHONIC is most convincing here as a trustworthy scientific institution. That normality matters. The later dystopia becomes more powerful precisely because it grows out of procedures, research ethics, standardized documentation and apparently reasonable attempts to improve human life.

“If life has a limit, then the limit can be studied.”Conceptual corporate archive statement
Can life be extended?
02
Advanced Prosthetics — Restoring the Body

We learned to restore the body.

Extending life was not enough if the body could no longer function.

As longevity research advanced, the company encountered a second limit. Saving a life did not necessarily restore what injury or disease had taken away. The research question moved from duration to function: not only how long a person could live, but how the body could return to use.

Advanced Prosthetics marks the transition from Life Science toward Human Engineering. Prosthetic limbs, artificial joints, mechanical interfaces and the relationship between nerve signals and machines force the company to deconstruct the body into functions that can be measured, substituted and reconnected.

This is a crucial conceptual change. Once the body becomes a system that can be repaired component by component, the next requirement is obvious: the system must first be read accurately. How does this body move? What signals does it produce? Which characteristics belong uniquely to this individual? The attempt to restore the body naturally opens the door to Biological Data Research.

Restore what life has lost.Verbal direction / Advanced Prosthetics
Can the body be restored?
03
Biological Data — Identifying Life

We learned to identify life.

To restore the body, TYTHONIC first had to learn how to read it.

Heart rate, blood chemistry, neural response, movement, tissue condition and recovery patterns become measurable variables. What begins as observation for treatment gradually becomes a highly structured body of data. Across enough subjects, recurring patterns and individual differences emerge — a biological signature capable of distinguishing one person from another.

The language of the institution changes with the technology. A patient can become a SUBJECT. Observation becomes DATA. A name and a face become only two identifiers among many. The same precision that allows TYTHONIC to understand a human being also allows that human being to be registered, compared, tracked and connected.

This is the first point where the world begins to feel uncomfortable without becoming visually chaotic. The laboratory remains bright. Documents remain clean. The typography remains neutral. Yet terms such as SUBJECT ID, BIOLOGICAL DATA and REGISTERED begin to appear. Protection and administration are now using the same infrastructure.

Every life has a signature.Verbal direction / Biological Data
Can life be identified?
04
Blue Chip — Preserving Identity

We learned to preserve identity.

Decades of research converge into a device small enough to fit in the hand.

Within this project, Blue Chip is treated not as an isolated game item but as a technological consequence of TYTHONIC’s history. If life has been studied, the body reconstructed and the individual identified biologically, then the next step is to preserve that identity and attach it to a system.

Once the prototype becomes manufacturable, research becomes industry. Chips are produced in batches, checked through quality control, organized into standardized trays and managed through labels, serial numbers and product classifications. At this point, corporate identity is no longer decorative branding. It becomes operational language — a way to distinguish ownership, status, access and reliability.

Blue Chip also creates a new dependency. Information that can be preserved must be transmissible. Transmission requires a transmitter. A transmitter requires communication infrastructure, servers, facilities, maintenance and authorization. A device became a network.

Life. Identified.Verbal direction / Blue Chip
Can identity be preserved?
05
The Battlegrounds — Managing Survival

And built a system for survival.

Eventually, every previous technology meets inside one operating environment.

Only here does the full structure become visible. TYTHONIC is no longer simply a life-science company, nor merely a sponsor whose logo happens to appear inside the Battlegrounds. Life research, prosthetics, biometric data, Blue Chip, transmitters, production facilities and communication infrastructure begin to read as components of the same operating system.

A Blue Chip alone cannot make the Battlegrounds function. Participants must be identified. Information must be transmitted. Equipment must be manufactured and maintained. Field communication must remain active. Security systems must define access. One product becomes a network, the network becomes infrastructure and infrastructure becomes part of the environment that makes the Battlegrounds operational.

TYTHONIC does not merely exist inside the Battlegrounds. Its technology helps make the Battlegrounds possible. That is where the company’s contradiction becomes most important. Technologies developed under the language of protecting life now make it possible to identify, connect and administer the state of human beings. The ability to manage life becomes the ability to manage survival.

Then, What Should This Company Look Like?

The stronger the narrative, the more restrained and consistent the identity should become.

TYTHONIC should not look like a conventional science-fiction brand decorated with obvious signals of “the future.” It is a corporation with a history stretching back to the 1970s, operating across life science, biometric data, industrial equipment, research facilities, security systems and field infrastructure. The same mark must create institutional trust on a scientific report, technical precision on a Blue Chip, durability on industrial equipment and immediate recognition on a container or factory wall.

The design problem is therefore not how to create more graphics, but how to make one visual rule survive across more environments without losing authority. The project keeps the existing symbol and TYTHONIC BLUE while refining proportion, combinations, typography, information hierarchy, verbal language and application logic. The goal is not a futuristic moodboard. It is the credibility of a corporation that already operates the future as routine infrastructure.

01TRUST

Institutional stability appropriate to a corporation entrusted with life, medical research and biological information.

02PRECISION

A repeatable information structure for technical documents, product labels and security systems.

03TECHNICAL

Technology expressed through structure, material and information rather than decorative futurism.

04CONSISTENCY

A single identity capable of linking laboratories, products, factories and field infrastructure.

Design Perspective / Visual Doctrine

The identity should not merely look futuristic.
It should look like an institution already operating the future.

TYTHONIC requires the visual language of a mature pharmaceutical and industrial corporation, not a technology startup trying to advertise innovation. A disciplined blue, generous white space, neutral sans-serif typography, reproducible grids and a strictly controlled master symbol communicate an organization whose authority comes from standards rather than spectacle.

01 / Institutional TrustClinical, not friendly.

Calm enough to reassure, but distant enough to preserve the authority of a research institution.

02 / Industrial RepeatabilitySame mark, every scale.

The symbol and color rules must reproduce consistently from a business card to a transmitter or container.

03 / Technical NeutralityInformation before decoration.

Product names, serials, access levels and divisions take priority over visual effects.

04 / Controlled ContrastBlue / White / Neutral.

Contrast and reversal create tension without multiplying unnecessary colors.

05 / Symbol DisciplineDo not reinvent the mark.

The existing geometry is treated as an asset; scale, spacing, placement and reversal are controlled instead.

06 / Dystopian RestraintThe fear comes from normality.

The more credible and institutional the system feels, the more unsettling its operational role becomes.

From Lore to Identity

Keep the identity. Refine the system.

This is not a rebrand intended to replace TYTHONIC’s existing identity. The project preserves the logo, color, products and environmental clues already present in the world-building, then details them into a Corporate Identity System capable of behaving like the identity of a real global corporation.

Logo Refinement→Color→Typography→Graphic System→Verbal Identity→Corporate / Product / Spatial
MASTER SYMBOL

Primary color #204FAB. Existing symbol geometry is treated as the master asset. Applications change scale, placement and positive / reverse color use — not the symbol itself.

Verbal Identity

One company. Two voices.

Externally, TYTHONIC speaks like a calm, human and confident global life-science corporation. Internally, equipment and security systems use short, emotionally neutral commands. The verbal system therefore preserves the same gap as the visual system: Public Face versus Operational Reality.

The corporate voice uses words such as continuity, humanity, protection and possibility. The system voice uses verbs: identify, register, connect, transmit, recall, authorize. One side gives meaning to the institution; the other turns that meaning into procedure.

Blue Chip System

A device became a network.

Blue Chip is developed as an ecosystem rather than as a single object: Prototype, Production, QC, Packaging, Technical Information, Transmitter and Field Infrastructure. This allows the technology to read as something a real corporation researches, manufactures, documents, deploys and maintains.

The system also demonstrates why CI consistency matters. The same corporate mark and information hierarchy must survive on a clean technical diagram, a manufactured component, a rugged field transmitter and the facilities that support it.

Corporate Applications

One identity, from the laboratory to the field.

The final test of a corporate identity is not how attractive the logo looks in isolation, but whether the same rules remain credible across every operational touchpoint. Corporate stationery, access systems, research uniforms, packaging, laboratories, Blue Chip production, industrial equipment, transmitters and spatial signage all repeat the same TYTHONIC BLUE, symbol geometry and information discipline.

TYTHONIC INDUSTRIES

ENGINEERING LIFE CONTINUITY.

The fragments of TYTHONIC’s world-building — history, language, technology and visual cues — are connected here as a coherent Corporate Identity System. The project does not replace the logo. It designs the world in which that logo can behave like the mark of a real institution.
